---
name: Market brand architecture
description: The approved relationship between United Main and the Stoneham Farmers Market visual systems.
---

The Stoneham Farmers Market should feel like a warm, neighborhood-facing sub-brand of United Main—not a visually separate product and not an identical copy of the parent site.

**Why:** The United Main site communicates a polished local digital platform, while the market needs the immediacy and community energy of its physical poster. Sharing a few recognizable parent-brand signals prevents the two experiences from feeling unrelated.

**How to apply:** Keep the United Main near-black header/footer, editorial serif voice, fine grid/rule details, and gold connective accents. Let the market content use a warm cream base with garden green, tomato red, bold condensed display type, and a script accent used sparingly. Do not combine the previous dark-teal market treatment with the red/green poster palette.
